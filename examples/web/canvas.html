texish, rendered to canvas

Same engine as the SVG demo, but drawn with texish.autoRenderCanvas(): ordinary text is painted with the browser's own hinted fillText, so it is as crisp as native text, and only the no-codepoint math glyphs are filled as canvas paths. Rendered at the device pixel ratio.

The mass-energy relation is $E = mc^2$, and the roots of $a x^2 + b x + c = 0$ are $$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4 a c}}{2 a}.$$
